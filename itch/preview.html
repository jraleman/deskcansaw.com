DeskCanSaw

Weird little games

Follow

Weird little games. I make small things, I take old games apart, and I put both up where people can find them.

Downloads and browser builds, all of them small. Some are finished; some prove one idea and then stop. The label on each one says which.

What the labels mean

  • released — done. Play it.
  • playable — you can finish it. Rough edges expected.
  • prototype — runs, proves one mechanic, ugly.
  • concept — an idea and maybe some art. Nothing to play yet.

I use them plainly. If a thing is a prototype I would rather say so than dress it up.

Start here

Desk Can Saw takes place on a DESK. Watch out for the sliding CANs. Slice them with a shmooving electric SAW ;) It is the one the whole name came from, so it may as well be the one you play first.

After that: Triangle Rush for a high score, Chicken Pit for tug-o-war against a friend, and Dead Metal Jam if you have an instrument, a voice, or anything else that makes noise.
